# Authored learning catalog

The study room previously exposed 20 fixed mini-lessons across seven subjects.
The much larger `exam-syllabus.ts` index supplied planning and AI topic options,
but did not contain lessons. AI provider credentials do not expand this static catalog.

This update adds 60 original mini-lessons and 60 matching worked-answer exercises,
for 80 lessons in 16 subjects. It is a preparatory foundation, not an exhaustive
course, an official question collection, or certification of mastery. Some topic
names describe broad syllabus categories; the objective states the narrower skill
actually introduced in each mini-lesson. No compulsory literary work is presented
as fully covered by these introductions.

## Authorship, origin and verification

New text, examples, distractors and answer explanations were authored for Kognos
with AI assistance on 2026-11-03. The repository's proprietary LICENSE applies.
No institution's questions, textbook passages or third-party course material were
copied. The institutional links identify curriculum orientation, not authorship or
endorsement of these lessons. The questions have not undergone independent teacher
review; structural tests do not establish pedagogical completeness.

Orientation follows the existing editorial index and these primary references:

- [INEP reference matrices](https://www.gov.br/inep/pt-br/centrais-de-conteudo/acervo-linha-editorial/publicacoes-institucionais/avaliacoes-e-exames-da-educacao-basica/matrizes-de-referencia-enem)
- [FUVEST program and candidate resources](https://www.fuvest.br/vestibular-da-usp)
- [UNESP candidate documents](https://vestibular.unesp.br/pagina/r45hom/documentos-vestibular-unesp-2026)
- [VUNESP examination portal](https://www.vunesp.com.br/)
- [ETEC official examination guidance](https://vestibulinho.etec.sp.gov.br/duvidas-frequentes/)
- [SEDUC SARESP explanation and grade distinctions](https://atendimento.educacao.sp.gov.br/knowledgebase/article/SED-08378/pt-br)

Primary institutional pages were located or consulted on 2026-11-03. Full FUVEST
and INEP PDF retrieval was unavailable in this session. The SEDUC reference describes
2025, and the linked UNESP documents describe 2026: do not infer a verified 2027
syllabus from them. Existing edition labels in the planning index are not newly
certified by this change. Confirm the applicable manual, grade, language option and
edition before claiming coverage. VUNESP is an organizer of different examinations,
not a single syllabus; ETEC and SARESP are not automatically assigned all upper
secondary subjects.

## Integration and compatibility

- Original lesson IDs and q01–q20 answer keys remain stable; new IDs are q21–q80.
- The initial diagnostic remains the original twenty items. Practice can use the
  larger bank; mock sessions are capped at twenty filtered items.
- New lesson exercises use the existing guest/cloud attempt and spaced-review paths.
- AI references are validated against exact subject/topic pairs. Explicit editorial
  bridges connect selected fundamentals to ETEC/SARESP. No fuzzy matching is used.
- Each lesson exposes its related institution references in a collapsible section.
- Progress fractions use the larger denominator. Historical answers are not deleted.
- No database migration or private key change is needed for static lessons.
- The twelve-minute labels on new lessons estimate a short study-and-practice session,
  not text-only reading time or measured student completion time.

Future expansion should add substantive lessons, exercises and reviewed mappings
for the remaining syllabus topics, rather than turning headings into empty lessons.
